CampusLink is the student app for Cavendish University Uganda. Sign in with your school account to get official notices, your timetable and exam dates, events, tutors and help from every office.

Sign in with your school account

Notices and news

Official announcements, course notices and campus news, with alerts so you don't miss them.

Timetable and exams

Your classes and exam dates, imported from the university's files, with reminders.

Events

See what's on, say you're going, and get a reminder an hour before.

Tutors and study groups

Find tutors for your units and study with classmates.

Help desks and campus map

Know which office handles what, and how to get there.

Safe by design

School accounts only, sign-in alerts, and a list of every device you're signed in on.

CampusLink works in your web browser and as an Android app. It is a student-built project for the Cavendish University Uganda community.
