Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

CampusLink is the student app for Cavendish University Uganda. This policy explains what information we keep, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have. We keep only what the app needs to work. We never sell your information and we show no advertising.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy
Account: your name, school email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google profile picture To create your account and make sure only members of the university join.
Profile: programme, year, nickname, interests and any details you choose to add To show you the right notices, timetable and events. Your student number is never shown to other users.
What you post: posts, comments, reactions, questions, event replies and study group messages To share them with the people you post them to.
Study information: timetables and exam dates you import or add To show your schedule and send reminders.
Settings: which alerts you want and your quiet hours To respect your choices.
Sign-in records: the type of device or browser, its network (IP) address and the time To warn you when a new device signs in and let you sign out other devices. Kept for 90 days.
Remembered devices: a random code kept on each phone or browser you sign in with (we store only a scrambled copy) So signing in again on a device you've used before doesn't trigger an alert. Signing a device out from "Your devices" forgets it.
Device code for phone alerts (Android app only) To deliver alerts when the app is closed. Removed when you sign out.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos you haven't shared, or anything from other apps.

2. Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with CampusLink. We ask for nothing more: no access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or contacts. We use this information only to sign you in and fill in your profile. CampusLink's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. Who can see your information

  • Other users see your name, photo, role badge and what you post. Your email, student number and sign-in records are private.
  • University staff using CampusLink (for example course lecturers or office staff) see what they need for their role, such as how many people have read a notice.
  • Our service providers process data only on our behalf, to run CampusLink:
    • Supabase: database, accounts and file storage (servers in Ireland, EU)
    • Cloudflare: hosting the website
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering phone alerts
    • Brevo: sending emails such as password resets and sign-in alerts
    • Google: "Sign in with Google", if you use it
  • We share information with authorities only when the law requires it.

4. How we protect it

All connections are encrypted. Database rules make sure each person can reach only what they are allowed to. Only school email addresses can create accounts, and you are alerted whenever your account is signed in on a new device. If you get an alert you don't recognise, open Me > Your devices or use the "This wasn't me" button in the email to sign out everywhere.

5. Keeping and deleting your information

  • We keep your information while your account is open.
  • You can close your account in Me > Close account. You have 30 days to change your mind; after that your account and personal information are deleted. Some posts may remain without your name where needed for others' conversations to make sense.
  • Sign-in records are deleted after 90 days.

6. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your profile, change which alerts you get, set quiet hours, sign out other devices and close your account at any time inside the app. You can also ask us for a copy of your information or to correct or delete it. Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 you may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Office.

7. Children

CampusLink is for university students and staff. It is not meant for children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: centient@campuslinks.online
