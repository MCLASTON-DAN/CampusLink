## What we collect, and why
| Data | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Name, programme, year, faculty | Show who you are to other students, find tutors and buddies | Signed-in students (never your student number) |
| Student number | Stop fake and duplicate accounts | You and platform staff |
| Timetable | Show your schedule | Only you |
| Help requests and messages | Get you help from an office | You and the office staff |
| Tutor profile, prices, contact you choose to share | Let students find tutors | Signed-in students |
| Comments, likes, reviews | Community and tutor credibility | Signed-in students |
| Survey answers | Improve the university | Stored apart from who answered; shown only as totals, and only after 5+ answers |
| Feedback | Improve the university | Anonymous (no user id stored) |

## What we do not do
We do not sell data. We do not show ads. We do not read your private requests
except to resolve them.

## Your choices
You can see, correct and delete your account data. Reviews you wrote stay but
lose your name. Contact [email] to ask.

## Keeping it safe
Everything is behind sign-in. Access rules are enforced in the database itself.
Official posts, removals and who did them are logged. Photos are limited to 1 MB.

## Changes
We will tell you in the app before this notice changes.
