# CampusLink Posting Code (DRAFT)

Everyone who posts for the Guild, the Administration or a club agrees to this
before publishing. Thousands of students read what you post. Be careful.

1. **Only post what you can back up.** Every news item needs a source note:
   the circular, memo, minute, or person who confirmed it.
2. **Stay in your lane.** Post about the university, its activities, development
   forums and things that help students. No personal, party-political, religious
   or commercial promotion.
3. **Check before you publish.** If you have not read it, verified it and
   understood it, do not post it. Posting just to post is not allowed.
4. **Correct mistakes in the open.** Edits are recorded. If you were wrong, post a
   clarification. Do not quietly rewrite history.
5. **Never name or shame a private person.** No student numbers, phone numbers,
   marks or personal details of anyone.
6. **Your term is limited.** Guild posting rights end on the date set when you
   were appointed (at most 12 months). Your badge ends the same day.
7. **Removals are public.** If a post is removed, the reason is shown to all
   students in the moderation log. Content is hidden, not secretly deleted.
8. **Posting for your office means your name is attached.** Your position is
   shown on every post you make.

Breaking this code can lead to loss of posting rights.
